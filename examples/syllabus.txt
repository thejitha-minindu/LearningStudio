AUTHORED DEMONSTRATION SYLLABUS — not an actual university exam specification

Database course scope:
1. Relational tables, primary keys, foreign keys and composite keys.
2. SQL INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN, matching rows and null-extended rows.
3. Functional dependencies, normalization, update anomalies and lossless decomposition.

Learning outcomes: explain the constraints represented by keys; predict query results
on small tables; reason about dependencies and justify simple decompositions.

No topic weighting, past-paper distribution, or exam-question predictions are supplied.
The two sample lecture PDFs cover only keys and joins; normalization needs supplementary evidence.
